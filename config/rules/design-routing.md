# Product design routing

Use `product-design` as the single design skill for a new or materially changed experience. Work in Figma through the configured bridge, implement the approved design, and check relevant visual/interaction drift. Preserve an explicit instruction to skip Figma and the emergency-fix exception.

Do not restart design for a nonvisual change, experience-neutral refactor, copy correction that preserves layout, or a bug/accessibility fix restoring approved behavior. A change that alters navigation, hierarchy or interaction still qualifies regardless of its filename or label.

Read only the skill references needed for the actual platform and decision. A multi-platform task may need multiple platform references; a single-platform task does not. Do not load a competing design skill. If the required skill/bridge is unavailable, report that dependency and continue independent non-design work; do not invent an approved design.
